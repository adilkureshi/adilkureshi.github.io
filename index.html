AML RIGHTSOURCE

Senior AML Analyst

I work on the Production Orders team for a major Canadian bank. A production order requires specific records to be provided. A preservation order requires records to be kept. I review the request to understand which accounts, dates and records it covers and what reporting requirements apply.

I review customer profiles, account records and transactions tied to the request. I trace incoming and outgoing funds, look for activity that differs from what we know about the customer, and document the evidence and any gaps.

I escalate concerns through the bank’s review process and draft Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) narratives. I also review other analysts’ case write-ups for clarity and accuracy.

  • Production and preservation orders
  • Transaction tracing
  • STR narratives
  • Peer quality review
